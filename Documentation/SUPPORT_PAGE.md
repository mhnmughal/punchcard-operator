About the App

Punchcard Operator is a fully offline, single player logic puzzle game. There is no account to set up and no internet connection required, so most connectivity issues do not apply to this app.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: The app looks locked to landscape orientation, is that a bug?
A: No. Punchcard Operator is designed to be played in landscape only, matching the punch card machine layout. Portrait mode is intentionally disabled.

Q: I lost my progress after reinstalling the app, can I get it back?
A: Progress is saved only on your own device and is not backed up to any account or cloud service, since the app collects no data and has no login. Reinstalling or switching devices will reset your progress.

Q: Does the app collect any of my data?
A: No. Punchcard Operator is fully offline and collects no personal data, analytics, or advertising identifiers of any kind. See our Privacy Policy page for details.

Q: How do I reset my progress?
A: Open Settings from the main menu and choose Reset All Progress.

Q: I found a bug or the app crashed, how do I report it?
A: Please email us at brucasse@icloud.com with your device model, iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened. Screenshots help a lot.

Reporting a Problem

If something isn't working as expected, email brucasse@icloud.com with as much detail as you can, including the level number if it happened during a specific puzzle.
