Punchcard Operator - Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

Operator Works ("we", "us") built Punchcard Operator as a fully offline, single player puzzle game.

Data Collection

Punchcard Operator does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, analytics data, advertising identifiers, or device information. The app has no account system, no login, no server connection, and no third party SDKs of any kind.

All game progress (which levels you have unlocked, your best rating per level, and your volume settings) is saved as a single file on your own device using iOS's standard local application storage. This data never leaves your device, is never transmitted anywhere, and is never seen by us.

Third Party Services

Punchcard Operator uses none. No analytics, no advertising networks, no crash reporting services, no cloud services of any kind.

Children's Privacy

Punchcard Operator does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes because the app's functionality changes, the updated version will be posted at this same page with a new effective date.
